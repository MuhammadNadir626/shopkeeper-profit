Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Welcome to SHOPKEEPER Profit Calculator. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use the app.

1. Information Collection

SHOPKEEPER Profit Calculator is a calculation application designed to help users calculate profit, loss, discounts, quantities, profit margins, and other business-related values.

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, phone number, or home address through the app. The app does not require users to create an account or log in.

2. How the App Works

Information entered into the calculator is used to perform calculations and display results.

The app provides calculation tools and allows users to view and manage their calculation history.

3. Data Storage

Calculation history and related app data are stored locally on the user's device. We do not operate our own server to collect or store this information.

Users can manage or clear their saved calculation history through the app.

4. Exporting Data

Users may choose to export their calculations as PDF or CSV files. These files are saved or shared through the device's available features at the user's direction.

The handling of exported files is subject to the user's device settings and chosen destination.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not use Google AdMob, Google Analytics, or third-party advertising or tracking services.

We do not share users' personal information with third-party services.

6. Data Security

We aim to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
